This is a realistic-proportioned adult female 3D model with clothing and a
ponytail, not a photograph or a photorealistic scan. It contains face/eye
morph targets, separate eyes, teeth and tongue, and an upper-body skeleton.
The first prototype should be assessed for naturalness before adding a room.

Other TalkingHead example avatars were not included, since their licensing
or styling does not fit this prototype.
